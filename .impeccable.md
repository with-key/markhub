## Design Context

### Users
`mdhub` is for PMs, POs, planners, designers, and developers working in small collaborative teams.
They use the product to keep epics, tasks, and markdown documents connected in one project context so planning intent and execution stay aligned.
The interface should support focused work, quick scanning, and low-friction navigation across structured project artifacts.

### Brand Personality
The brand personality is simple, concise, and clean.
The product should feel calm, trustworthy, and highly legible rather than expressive or decorative.
It should help teams feel organized and in control without looking cold or over-engineered.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a minimal, high-clarity visual system inspired by clean SaaS product surfaces such as ElevenLabs' sign-in experience, especially its restraint, spacing discipline, and low-noise composition.
Prefer light and dark mode parity, with strong readability in both themes.
Favor a restrained palette with one clear accent color and avoid interfaces that feel busy, overly colorful, playful, or visually fragmented.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize clarity over decoration.
Every screen should be understandable at a glance, with strong hierarchy, obvious actions, and minimal visual clutter.

2. Keep the interface quiet and disciplined.
Use a restrained color system, generous whitespace, and a small number of component variants to maintain consistency.

3. Optimize for legibility and scanability.
Typography, spacing, contrast, and grouping should make structured project information easy to read and compare quickly.

4. Build polished simplicity, not blandness.
The UI should feel intentional and refined, with clean proportions and subtle visual depth, while avoiding noisy gradients, excessive ornament, or template-like sameness.

5. Maintain parity across light and dark themes.
Both modes should preserve hierarchy, contrast, and calmness without relying on dramatically different visual identities.
